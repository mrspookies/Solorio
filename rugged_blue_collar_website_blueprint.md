# Blueprint: Rugged & Rustic Handyman/Carpentry Website for OpenCode

This guide outlines the tech stack, aesthetic choices, and precise prompt to feed your OpenCode agent to build a modern, high-end, yet rugged website for a Gen X blue-collar master craftsman.

---

## 1. Recommended Tech Stack
*   **The Framework:** **Next.js** (React) or **Vite + Astro** (ideal for fast, lightweight static business brochure sites).
*   **The Styling Toolkit:** **Tailwind CSS** combined with **shadcn/ui** or custom Tailwind utility classes to handle animations and layout seamlessly.

---

## 2. The Aesthetic & Design System
To capture a genuine blue-collar, high-end artisanal vibe instead of a sterile corporate tech look, enforce these design cues:
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   *Backgrounds:* Deep charcoal and slate tones (resembling weathered steel or dark asphalt).
    *   *Accents:* Warm, rich wood tones (burnt oak, walnut, cedar amber).
    *   *Call-to-Action (CTA):* High-visibility safety orange or brass yellow for buttons that pop.
*   **Typography:** 
    *   *Headers:* Bold, blocky, industrial fonts (e.g., *Bebas Neue* or heavy industrial sans-serifs).
    *   *Body:* Ultra-clean, legible text to ensure high readability on mobile devices.
*   **Textures & Visuals:** Subtle grid lines, distressed border styles, faint brushed-metal gradients, and heavy drop shadows to give the layout physical weight and depth.

---

## 3. The OpenCode Prompt
Copy and paste this prompt directly into your workspace agent to generate the site:

> *"Build a rugged, rustic, and modern landing page for a master carpenter and general contractor using Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and TypeScript. The aesthetic should be blue-collar, bold, and high-end—utilizing deep charcoal and slate tones, warm cedar/amber wood accents, and high-visibility safety-orange call-to-action buttons.*
> 
> *Include the following sections:*
> *1. **Hero Section:** A bold industrial headline (e.g., 'Built Right. Built to Last.'), a subtitle emphasizing decades of craftsmanship, and a prominent 'Get a Free Estimate' button.*
> *2. **The Craft (Services Grid):** A dark-card grid highlighting Custom Framing, Finish Carpentry, Remodeling & Additions, and Heavy Repairs, with rugged hover effects.*
> *3. **The Portfolio (Before/After & Project Gallery):** An interactive filterable gallery showcasing woodwork, decks, and structural builds with high-impact image layouts.*
> *4. **About the Craftsman:** A section highlighting decades of hands-on experience, local pride, and old-school work ethic.*
> *5. **Quote Request / Contact Form:** A straightforward, no-nonsense form asking for project type, timeline, and phone number, styled like a rugged job intake sheet."*

---

## 4. Key Features to Request
1.  **Before/After Image Sliders:** Essential for carpentry and remodeling to showcase dramatic transformations.
2.  **Trust Badges & Service Areas:** Clear highlights for licensing, insurance, and local municipal coverage.
3.  **Mobile-First Optimization:** Ensures clients browsing on their phones while at home get a seamless experience.
4.  **Active Status Ticker:** A small real-time badge indicating current scheduling availability (e.g., *"Currently booking local framing & interior remodeling"*).
